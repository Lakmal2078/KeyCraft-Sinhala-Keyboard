Privacy & Open Source

KeyCraft is built to make Sinhala typing useful without ads. This page describes the app behaviour documented in the project and its source code.

At a glance
  • Free and open source under GNU GPL-3.0.
  • No advertisements are included in the app.
  • Sinhala transliteration is performed on your device.
  • Internet access is used to download optional dictionaries when you request them.

Keyboard input and transliteration

Singlish-to-Sinhala transliteration is processed locally on your Android device. The project does not describe sending the text you type to a remote transliteration service.

Network access and dictionaries

KeyCraft requests internet access so you can download optional dictionaries. A dictionary download contacts the configured dictionary source. You can choose whether to use this optional feature.

Clipboard features

Clipboard History and clipboard previews are optional features controlled through the app's settings. Review those settings and Android clipboard permissions before enabling clipboard-related features. Do not paste sensitive information into apps unless you intend to share it.

Ads, analytics and tracking

KeyCraft contains no ads. This page makes no broader claim about third-party Android services or the behaviour of other apps installed on your device; review the source and your device settings for details relevant to your setup.

Backups

KeyCraft limits Android Backup and device-to-device transfer strictly to your keyboard configuration settings (com.lakmal.keycraftsinhala_preferences.xml). Learned Sinhala predictions, downloaded dictionaries, recent emojis, cached files, and in-memory clipboard history are excluded from backups and stay on your device.

Open-source code and license

You can inspect, build, modify and contribute to KeyCraft using the public repository. KeyCraft is distributed under the GNU General Public License version 3.0 (GPL-3.0).
