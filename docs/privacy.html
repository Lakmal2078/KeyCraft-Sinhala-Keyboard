Privacy

  • On-device transliteration: Singlish to Sinhala conversion runs on your phone.
  • Optional dictionaries: the app uses the internet only when you choose to download extra dictionaries.
  • Clipboard: clipboard history and preview features are controlled in Settings. Check them before enabling.
  • No ads: KeyCraft does not show advertisements.
  • No cloud backup of your data: keyboard preferences and app data are not backed up to the cloud or transferred between devices.
