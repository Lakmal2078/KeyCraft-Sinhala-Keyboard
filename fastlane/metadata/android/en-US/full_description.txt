KeyCraft is a free and open-source Sinhala keyboard for Android. Type in Singlish and convert it to Sinhala with on-device transliteration, word suggestions, customizable layouts and themes.

FEATURES

• Sinhala transliteration: type in Singlish and get Sinhala text as you type.
• Word suggestions: use bundled Sinhala and English dictionaries for word completion.
• Swipe typing: swipe towards key corners to enter additional characters and symbols.
• Custom layouts: choose keyboard layouts and configure extra keys.
• Themes and appearance: customize colors, key shapes, borders, fonts, spacing and keyboard size.
• Productivity tools: quick calculator, text shortcuts and quick-action toolbar.
• Clipboard tools: optional clipboard history and quick paste.
• Typing controls: number row, numpad, key repeat, auto-capitalization and vibration settings.

PRIVACY

• Sinhala transliteration is processed on your device.
• KeyCraft contains no advertisements or analytics trackers.
• Internet access is used only when you choose to download optional dictionaries.
• Keyboard input is not sent to a server.

KeyCraft is based on Unexpected Keyboard and is released under GNU GPL-3.0. The source code and third-party notices are available from the project website.
